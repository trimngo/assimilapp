# German Study Companion

An offline iPhone-friendly companion for your German Assimil course. Import your book PDFs and audio once; files and progress stay on the device. The hosted app contains only software and prepared lesson titles, page mappings, and timing metadata.

## Start studying

Open [the app](https://trimngo.github.io/assimilapp/dist/) in Safari, choose Share → Add to Home Screen, then import inside the installed app. Safari tabs and Home Screen apps may have separate storage.

In **Menu → My materials**, select your full `ASSIMIl German book.pdf` and lesson MP3s. The supplied `001-300.pdf`, `301-600.pdf`, and `601-614.pdf` still work as an alternative. Imports can be split into batches. Start with recordings 1–3 if you prefer. The app checks the full book’s 614-page layout once (keep it open until the check finishes), or verifies the split originals by fingerprint. It automatically finds each lesson's pages, and matches audio without assigning recordings manually. Keep the original materials and export progress occasionally; iOS storage retention is not guaranteed.

Choose a lesson and follow the eight steps. The lesson number and title appear above the current instruction. The compact top toolbar holds lesson selection and the menu; Back and Next appear below the lesson content. Only the current instruction and relevant controls appear; materials, backup, and updates live in the menu.

The countdown shares the existing toolbar row. Suggested step lengths are **5, 3, 7, 5, 10, 8, 5, and 2 minutes** (45 minutes total). Each step starts its timer; tap the countdown to pause or resume, or long press a paused timer to reset and restart. Running timers are green; after zero they stay running, turn red and count overtime with a +. Paused timers are gray. The timer pauses on menu pages. It keeps accurate elapsed time while the phone sleeps and across reloads, works offline, and lets playback and studying continue when time is up. Change the eight durations in **Menu → Settings → Study timer**, where you can also reset the current timer or restore the suggestions. Saved durations are included in progress backups.

1. **Review:** play the previous two or three available lesson dialogues in order.
2. **Blind listening:** play the current dialogue twice with the book hidden, then stop. Lesson playback starts with the spoken lesson number and title. Each listen includes the fast reading and slower repeat where the recording contains both, ending before exercises. Other study steps retain the first dialogue ending.
3. **Understand:** read the German, English translation, and notes on the mapped pages.
4. **Read along:** listen to the dialogue while following the pages. A compact playback bar appears when the main player scrolls out of view.
5. **Shadowing (10 minutes):** check the sentences you want to practice, then Play/Pause the selected set. Repeat is a checkbox, with an optional delay after each set. Adjacent selections retain the original recording’s pauses. Switch between German text and English prompts without losing the selection or playback position. Uniform page-width sentence strips keep the same text scale. Beside each sentence, **Orig** plays just the original sentence, **Rec** records your voice (tap **Stop** to save), and **Mine** plays your latest take. Recording again replaces that sentence’s take after a successful save. Allow microphone access when asked. Recording pauses lesson audio and repeating; switching steps or hiding the app stops the microphone and saves the take. Voice takes stay locally on this device and play offline, using the same Standard/Compatibility playback choice as lesson audio. They are not included in JSON progress backups and will be lost if website data is cleared. The numbered pencil beside each checkbox opens its timing editor; other playback and book tools stay under **Options**. Checkbox selections, language, repeat settings, custom timings and playback position save offline and are included in progress backups. The 10-minute timer covers the entire step.
6. **Exercises (8 minutes):** do the lesson’s exercises with the pages visible and the full recording available, including exercises.
7. **Write (5 minutes):** copy 4–5 sentences by hand, or write them from the audio as dictation. Both lesson pages and audio are available.
8. **Recall:** see all the prepared English translation crops; reveal and hide the lesson to check your German.

Lesson and study step save automatically. Wait for **App available offline**, then try closing and reopening in Airplane Mode. Imported materials are read from local storage each session, without uploading or importing them again.

## See your progress

**Menu → Progress** shows a month calendar, overall completed lessons, and this week's lesson count (Monday–Sunday). Press **Finish lesson** after Recall to record completion on your device's local date and move to the next lesson. Each lesson counts once per day, so repeating one on another day adds activity while the overall total counts it once. Tap a calendar day to see the lesson titles; browse months or return to Today. **Resume lesson** returns to your saved lesson and step. Finished lessons display a **✓ Completed** tag. Finishing lesson 100 opens Progress and keeps the final lesson available to revisit.

History starts when this feature is installed. **Manage completed lessons** lets you mark earlier lessons individually or through a chosen lesson number, without inventing calendar dates. Unmarking a lesson removes its completion tag and recorded calendar activity. These records work offline and travel with your progress backup; older backups preserve your existing completion history.

## Prepared course and custom materials

The [course index and preprocessing guide](course/README.md) cover all 100 lessons, 208 reviewed English recall crops, recording fingerprints, dialogue ranges, and 1,153 prepared cuts for 1,161 numbered dialogue items. Each primary Shadowing cut targets one numbered item, even when that item contains several grammatical sentences. The app uses those small metadata files to seek within original MP3s; it does not run speech recognition or detect pauses again for known recordings. Compatibility playback decodes the current recording to play it through Web Audio.

Shadowing text uses 1,161 prepared sentence crop coordinates and the PDF already stored on the device. Only the active practice range is rendered in the compact view. Each needed source page is rendered once per text update, then cropped into sentence excerpts. This avoids displaying the scan's OCR mistakes or publishing its dialogue text or images. Full books use global pages; split originals use their matching local pages. Timing corrections retain the book numbering when counts match; merged or split cuts use overlapping prepared items and can show a shared book item. Both text and playback work offline. Without-text passes hide both the excerpts and the full lesson pages.

Sentence text regression: run `node scripts/check_shadow_text.mjs /path/to/001-300.pdf /path/to/L010-LESSON.mp3 /path/to/L011-LESSON.mp3 /path/to/L058-LESSON.mp3 /path/to/full-book.pdf` with the browser/server overrides below. The full PDF is optional. It checks excerpt mappings, individual playback in Options, fixed selection, edited timings, phone/tablet layouts, missing parts, full-book references and offline restart.

Counts match in 92 lessons. Eight lessons each retain one merged pair; Shadowing warns when the active cuts differ from the book's count, including for custom timing lists. Automatic timing checks passed for 26 lessons. There are 160 flagged boundaries across 74 lessons; draft cuts can be adjusted. Automatic alignment is not a manual listening review. The first three lessons have independently checked sentence endpoints. Saved custom timings take priority over updated prepared defaults; extra custom splits are allowed.

Split PDFs and recordings are identified by SHA-256; renamed originals still match. A full PDF is recognized by checking all 614 pages against prepared text and position fingerprints. Small glyph and punctuation changes are tolerated, while changed page order or text placement refuses automatic mapping. The verified result is saved with the local file, so reopening does not repeat the check. A PDF without the expected text layer or layout needs custom page settings; alternative recordings need their own timings. No Google sign-in or OAuth setup is required. Save Drive files to Files on your phone, then choose them with the existing import picker.

For other books, **Book options** retains custom page numbers, optional page patterns, and English-area selection. **Playback options** allows manual audio choices. Existing file identities, page mappings, custom timings, and progress remain stored. Known originals use prepared cuts by default; previous timing lists can be selected with **Use saved custom timings**. New timing edits take precedence until you switch back. Choose **Use automatically matched pages** or **Use prepared sentence timings** to switch back from custom settings. Local pause detection is available only on request for other recordings. Recall refuses an uncropped whole page when there is no English-only area.

In landscape on any device, lesson pages use the full available width with 8-pixel side gutters that respect screen safe areas and an 8-pixel gap between pages. Page layout follows the reader panel's width: below 900 CSS pixels, pages stack one at a time; wider panels can show a spread. Landscape orientation does not force two columns. **Show two pages when there’s room** in Book options enables automatic spreads; uncheck it to keep one page even in a wide window.

Reader layout regression: run `node scripts/check_reader_layout.mjs /path/to/001-300.pdf` with the browser/server overrides below. It checks live landscape and portrait resizing, a reader narrower than the viewport, full-width single pages, the manual override, display diagnostics and offline reopening. If BOOX shrinks the whole interface, its browser or system may be scaling a desktop-sized viewport instead of reporting a smaller one. Check Desktop site/PC mode in the browser. **Menu → Help & updates → Copy display details** reports layout/visual viewport sizes, scale and recent resize events to distinguish browser scaling from reader layout.

## Updates and backups

In **Menu → Help & updates**, check the running version and choose **Check for updates** while online, then **Reload app** when a release is ready. Updates preserve IndexedDB and OPFS materials and progress, replacing only the app's shell cache. Offline checks show the running version without claiming it is the latest.

In **Menu → My materials → Backup & storage**, export or restore progress, completion history, timer durations, custom book settings, audio choices, and timing edits. Backups exclude media files. Import the original files before restoring so settings reconnect by filename and size. Version 1 and 2 progress backups remain supported; older backups keep your current timer settings and completion history.

## Implementation and checks

Buildless HTML/JavaScript. Large files stream into OPFS when supported, with an IndexedDB blob fallback. PDF.js 4.10.38 and its dependencies are bundled offline. A local range transport reads PDF slices without copying the entire book into an application buffer; canvases have bounded resolution. PDF evaluation is disabled. Multiple PDFs are opened lazily, including lesson 57's split-source pages. Full books use the same global page references directly. File hashing uses bounded 1 MB chunks; layout recognition reads the existing PDF text layer without OCR or image rendering.

`dist/course-index.json` mirrors `course/assimil-german.index.json`; `dist/combined-layout.json` mirrors the prepared layout fingerprints in `course/combined-layout.json`. The incremental hash implementation is bundled from @noble/hashes 1.8.0 under its MIT license. Copy the reviewed source into `dist/` when changing prepared data. The service worker installs the app shell atomically, including the course metadata, and never caches imported materials. `version.json` stays network-only. For releases, update `version.json`, `updates.js`, `sw.js`, and the HTML badge; increment matching shell cache names in `updates.js` and `sw.js`.

Run `npm test` for timing validation, fingerprint matching, full-book and split-PDF references, layout validation, bounded file hashing, complete recall coverage, book-count comparisons, and prepared timing padding. Run `python -m unittest discover -s tests -p 'test_*.py'` to check numbered-item extraction and alignment, and `python scripts/check_course.py` to validate the preprocessing output. Browser checks use the supplied PDFs and recordings to exercise automatic import, dialogue repeats, sentence playback, recall, split-source rendering, backup/restore, and offline restart. Actual iPhone testing remains necessary.

Sentence playback regression: serve the repository locally, install Playwright, then run `node scripts/check_sentence_playback.mjs /path/to/L010-LESSON.mp3`. This checks fixed sentence selection while scrubbing, the lesson 10 timing correction, free Play versus bounded Repeat/Preview, compact controls, automatic continuation, and offline retention. `STUDY_URL`, `PLAYWRIGHT_MODULE`, and `CHROMIUM_PATH` can override the server URL and browser installation.

The main audio player has compact Play/Pause, a seek bar, and elapsed/total time. Both playback views read the audio element's actual position, with a check every quarter second while playing as well as media events. Review limits do not depend on receiving a seek-complete event; source changes reset pending seeks. This keeps dialogue stops and displayed positions working when a browser delays or misses media notifications. Custom sentence selection and saved timings remain independent of the playhead.

## Shadowing sentence selection and compatibility endpoints

Compatibility audio schedules sentence and practice-range stops directly on the Web Audio output clock. Delayed page timers cannot let a scheduled range continue into the next sentence. Standard audio keeps the existing playback timers and clock checks. Reconnect rearms the active range; freely scrubbing clears it. Audio diagnostics include the scheduled stop.

Shadowing shows every dialogue item with a playback checkbox and an Edit button. Its German/English switch changes only the visible prompts: checked sentences, audio position and repeat settings stay in place, and each lesson remembers its language. English crops are prepared from the private PDF for all 100 lessons, with neighbouring inline review translations blanked using coordinate masks. Only coordinates are shipped; text and book images stay in the imported scan. Play/Pause uses the checked sentences in dialogue order. Adjacent checked sentences play continuously with the original recorded pauses. Before skipping unchecked speech, playback keeps the recorded pause preceding that speech, then moves to the next checked item. Pausing inside an original gap preserves that position. Repeat is a checkbox that loops the complete selected sequence. Its delay selector adds a pause of 0–60 seconds between complete repeats, with no added gaps inside the set. Pause or unchecking Repeat cancels a pending repeat. Selection changes start the updated set from the beginning on the next Play; Repeat changes apply at the next sequence end. Each lesson/recording keeps its selection, language, repeat setting and delay, current sentence and audio position locally and in progress backups. Leaving the step pauses playback. Timing repairs, recording settings and full pages remain in Options; each Edit button opens the timing editor beside its sentence. Excerpts retain the source page’s complete horizontal width and crop only vertically, keeping the scale and alignment consistent. Existing custom timings and legacy backups remain supported.

Run `node scripts/check_shadow_selection.mjs /path/to/001-300.pdf /path/to/L011-LESSON.mp3` with `STUDY_URL`, `PLAYWRIGHT_MODULE`, and `CHROMIUM_PATH` overrides for a local browser. It checks nonadjacent selections, entire-sequence looping in both engines, row editing, consistent scan scale, state retention across steps, backups, offline reopening and narrow controls.

Native multi-sentence playback keeps its endpoint when a browser delays a Play event or leaves the seeking flag set. Only a user action on the advanced seek bar releases the endpoint; delayed media events do not change playback intent. `scripts/check_shadow_boundaries.mjs /path/to/L011-LESSON.mp3` injects those delayed events and stale flags against actual native audio. This is a browser regression check, not a physical iPhone test.

English prompts and original pauses: `node scripts/check_shadow_language.mjs /path/to/001-300.pdf /path/to/L011-LESSON.mp3 /path/to/L007-LESSON.mp3` checks both audio engines, switching text during playback, pausing/resuming inside an original gap, editor selection independence, inline review masks, per-lesson settings, backups and offline use. Run `python3 scripts/prepare_english_excerpts.py part1.pdf part2.pdf part3.pdf` only with the exact private source parts to regenerate the English coordinates; no OCR text or images are written to the repository.

Voice recording regression: run `node scripts/check_voice_recording.mjs /path/to/001-300.pdf /path/to/L011-LESSON.mp3` with the same server/browser overrides. Chromium provides a synthetic microphone; capture, audio encoding/decoding, IndexedDB storage and playback use real browser APIs. The check covers one-take replacement, denied microphone access, bounded Original comparison, Standard/Compatibility playback, timing edits, navigation, language/state retention, compact side controls and offline reopening. Actual microphone permissions and Bluetooth routing still need testing on iPhone/BOOX.
